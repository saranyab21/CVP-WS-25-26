Q5.1.1
Felzenszwalb generates fine-grained, arbitrarily shaped segments, but Selective Search groups these into hierarchical, larger regions focused on object proposals, making it more suitable for object detection.

Q5.1.2:
Criteria in main.py:

Duplicate Removal: Avoid redundant boxes.
Minimum Size (< 2000): Filters small regions.
Aspect Ratio (w/h > 1.2 or h/w > 1.2): Removes elongated/narrow boxes.
Effect: Improves proposal quality. Additional criteria could include region overlap, compactness, and color variance.

Q5.1.3:
Rectangles are derived by calculating the bounding box of merged regions using their minimum and maximum x/y coordinates.
